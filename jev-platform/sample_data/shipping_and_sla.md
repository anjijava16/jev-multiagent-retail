# Shipping and Delivery

## Shipping options
Standard shipping takes 4 to 6 business days and is free on orders over $50. Express shipping takes 2 business days and costs $14.99. Orders placed before 2 PM Eastern on a business day ship the same day.

## Order processing
Orders in "processing" status have been paid but not yet handed to a carrier. Processing normally finishes within 1 business day. High-value orders over $1,000 can take up to 2 business days for fraud review.

## Late deliveries
If a shipped order is more than 3 business days past its estimated delivery date, customers can request a shipping refund. Support should open a carrier trace before issuing a replacement.

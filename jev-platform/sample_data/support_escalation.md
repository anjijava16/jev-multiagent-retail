# Support Escalation Procedure

## When to escalate to a human
Escalate to a human agent when the customer requests a refund above $500, reports a safety issue (overheating, battery swelling, burning smell), disputes a charge, or asks to close their account.

## Ticket priorities
Use "high" priority for safety issues and devices that will not power on. Use "normal" for everything else. Never promise a refund amount before a human has reviewed a high-value case.

## Response targets
High priority tickets get a first response within 4 business hours. Normal priority tickets get a first response within 1 business day.

# Returns and Refunds Policy

## Standard return window
Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method. Items must be unused and in original packaging.

## Electronics
Electronics (laptops, headphones, tablets, smart home devices) have a 15-day return window starting from the delivery date. Opened electronics are subject to a 10% restocking fee unless the item is defective. Laptops must be returned with all accessories and with any user accounts removed.

## Apparel and footwear
Apparel and footwear can be returned within 45 days. Shoes must show no outdoor wear.

## Non-returnable items
Gift cards, downloadable software, and personalised items cannot be returned.

## Refund timing
Refunds are issued within 5 business days after the warehouse receives and inspects the return. Card refunds can take an additional 3 to 7 days to appear on a statement.

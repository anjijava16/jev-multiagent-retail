# Warranty Coverage

## Manufacturer warranty
All electronics carry a 1-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. The warranty does not cover accidental damage, liquid damage, or normal battery wear.

## Extended protection plan
Customers can add a 2-year extended protection plan within 30 days of purchase. The plan costs 12% of the item price and covers accidental damage, with a limit of two claims.

## How to file a claim
Customers file a warranty claim through support. Support must collect the order id, a description of the defect, and photos. A support ticket with priority "high" is required for any device that will not power on.
